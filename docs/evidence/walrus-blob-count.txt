THE REST OF THE ACCOUNT
-----------------------
The MemWalAccount also holds records written by the same operator while building and
testing the app: books created to exercise language switching, currency handling,
reminder scheduling and the queueing path. They are the same agent writing to the
same account, and they are not evidence of real use.

The account total is therefore higher than 54. It is deliberately not used as the
real-use figure in the write-up, because most of it came from the build.

SCOPE
-----
Everything counted here is Memoranda. The operator's other projects use different
namespace names and were probed directly: graywake, graywake:main, worldremembers,
memora, memora:main, companion, leah, canon and default all returned total=0 under
this account.

METHOD
------
MemWalSync.recall(<query>, limit=1000, namespace='fieldpack:<pack_id>').total
returns the number of memories stored in that namespace on Walrus mainnet.

Namespaces were enumerated from the project's own records: every pack_id in
data/packs.json, unioned with every local journal file. 49 namespaces, 49 of 49
answered.

Note: the SDK's restore() summed over the same namespaces returns a much smaller
number. It counts what the relayer has indexed and is a lower bound, not a total.
Treat it as a lower bound. The two related defects are filed as issues on
MystenLabs/MemWal.
